How to Play — Lumina Studio

Lumina is designed as a **stress-free cognitive sanctuary**. There are no ticking countdown clocks while you solve—take as long as you need to visualize and reconstruct each pattern.

1. Zen Memory (Pattern & Harmony)

Study the glowing constellation of tiles during the Observation phase. Once the board clears, tap the tiles to recreate the exact pattern. From Level 3+, warm Amber tiles join Teal tiles—use the Brush selector or tap a tile multiple times to cycle colors!

2. Mind Shift (Spatial Rotation & Mirroring)

A deeper spatial workout! Observe the reference pattern and note the transformation rule shown in the banner:
• Mirror Horizontally (↔): Flip Left and Right columns.
• Mirror Vertically (↕): Flip Top and Bottom rows.
• Rotate 90° Clockwise (↻): Top row becomes the Right column.

3. Sequence Flow (Ordered Path)

Tiles illuminate one by one accompanied by ascending pentatonic chimes. Reconstruct the path by tapping the tiles in the exact order (or Reverse Order on advanced levels!).

Mindful Peek: Feeling stuck during the solve phase? Use one of your Mindful Peek charges to briefly glimpse the target pattern again. You earn +1 recharge every 3 constellations mastered.
